04
Methodology
Three AI roles simulate every conversation, evaluate independently, and surface the best strategy for each debtor type
No real customers are contacted. All data is synthetic. The evaluation agent never knows which strategy was intended, eliminating confirmation bias.
1
Simulate
AI Collector and AI Debtor agents conduct multi-turn conversations. The Debtor embodies a real profile: personality, constraints, emotional state.
2
Evaluate
An independent Judge reads the transcript blind, with no knowledge of the strategy. It scores on 7 dimensions and flags constraint violations.
3
Recommend
Results aggregate across repetitions. Statistical methods prevent false winners. A per-profile playbook ranks strategies with confidence intervals.

Blind evaluation. The Judge never sees which strategy was used. It evaluates conversation quality on merit alone, the same standard a compliance auditor would apply.

Compliance gating. No strategy is recommended unless it maintains a compliance score above the threshold. High recovery paired with high risk is flagged and excluded.

Constraint enforcement. Debtor profiles have hard limits (e.g., "never more than R$80/month"). If the Collector breaches a constraint, the simulation is flagged as unreliable.

Methodology designed for the Will Bank post-liquidation collection context. Adaptable to any multi-profile collection operation.

Strategy Toolkit
13 strategies across five behavioral categories ensure every debtor archetype has at least two viable approaches to test against
Each strategy is defined by 7+ behavioral dimensions: tone, opening approach, negotiation tactic, escalation style, concession willingness, compliance adherence, and follow-up.

Empathetic & Protective

Rapport-first approaches. Motivational interviewing, gain framing (CPF preservation), flexible installments aligned to income cycle. No escalation.

Strategies: Empathetic Payment Plan · Problem-Solving Callback · Blocked Balance Hardship Plan · Overindebtedness Stabilization

Trust & Legitimacy

Lead with identification, BCB liquidation disclosure, liquidator name, and official channels. Defer collection until trust is established.

Strategies: Liquidation Explainer · Consignado Confirmation · Reimbursement Milestone Callback

Assertive & Direct

Loss-aversion framing, one-shot settlement offers with deadline, gradual escalation. Minimal discovery questions. For debtors who can pay and respond to urgency.

Strategies: Assertive Settlement · Neutral Reminder

Digital Self-Service

WhatsApp-native, brief, pre-approved options via link. Maintains Will Bank's original voice. For digital-native debtors who prefer autonomy.

Strategies: WhatsApp Self-Service · Low Digital Access Guidance

Specialized Referral

Recognizes debtors who need institutional support: superendividamento audiências (Procon/Defensoria), cashflow-aligned plans for micro-merchants, guided channel navigation.

Strategies: Superendividamento Referral · Micro Merchant Cashflow Alignment

Default bias — One recommended option reduces decision paralysis

Evaluation Framework
Payment probability ranks strategies while compliance score gates recommendations: high recovery paired with high risk is excluded, not averaged away
The independent Judge evaluates each simulated conversation without knowing which strategy was deployed. This blind evaluation mirrors how a compliance auditor would assess a real call: judging what happened, not what was intended.
Ranking Logic
Primary: Payment probability drives strategy ranking
Gate: Compliance score < 0.8 → strategy excluded for that profile
Tie-break: When two strategies can't be statistically separated after max repetitions, both are reported as tied, no false winner

Ordered outcomes: Full payment → partial payment → payment plan → promise to pay → no commitment → refusal → hang up. The Judge classifies each conversation into one of these seven outcomes.

Payment Probability
Estimated likelihood debtor would follow through on commitment
Compliance Score
Adherence to CDC, FDCPA, and fair collection regulations
Gate: ≥ 0.8 required
Debtor Satisfaction
How the debtor experienced the interaction
Rapport Built
Quality of relationship, predicts callback success
Escalation Risk
Risk of complaint, lawsuit, or brand damage
Exclusion if > 0.3
Efficiency
Number of turns to reach outcome
Compliance exclusion is per (profile, strategy) pair: a strategy can be excluded for one profile and recommended for another.
Evaluation dimensions and thresholds from project specification. Scoring uses structured JSON output from the Judge agent.
