The Challenge
The liquidation created 14 distinct debtor situations — a single collection script cannot serve a mother in Fortaleza and a scam-suspicious professional in São Paulo
Debtors differ on financial capacity, emotional state, trust level, digital access, and hard constraints. Deploying untested strategies risks compliance violations, brand damage, and lost recovery.
Hardship profiles need protection, not pressure
Blocked salaries, benefit-dependent households, and over-indebted families (Lei 14.181) require vulnerability-aware approaches that respect the mínimo existencial.
Trust deficit is the first barrier, not willingness to pay
Post-liquidation scam alerts (FGC, Procon-SP) mean skeptical debtors will hang up before hearing the offer — unless legitimacy is established first.
Informed debtors negotiate, not comply
CDC art. 42-aware disputers, Feirão veterans demanding 70%+ discounts, and micro-merchants with irregular cashflow each require specialized playbooks.
Wrong strategy × wrong profile = compliance risk
Assertive tactics on vulnerable profiles create FDCPA/CDC exposure. Empathetic approaches on strategic negotiators leave recovery on the table.
The Core Question
Which strategy works best for which debtor — and how do we know before we deploy?
Real-world A/B testing is too slow, too risky, and ethically constrained. We need a way to test every strategy against every profile at scale — with zero customer exposure.

03
Methodology
Three AI roles simulate every conversation, evaluate independently, and surface the best strategy for each debtor type
No real customers are contacted. All data is synthetic. The evaluation agent never knows which strategy was intended — eliminating confirmation bias.
1
Simulate
AI Collector and AI Debtor agents conduct multi-turn conversations. The Debtor embodies a real profile — personality, constraints, emotional state.
2
Evaluate
An independent Judge reads the transcript blind — no knowledge of the strategy. It scores on 7 dimensions and flags constraint violations.
3
Recommend
Results aggregate across repetitions. Statistical methods prevent false winners. A per-profile playbook ranks strategies with confidence intervals.

Blind evaluation. The Judge never sees which strategy was used. It evaluates conversation quality on merit alone — the same standard a compliance auditor would apply.

Compliance gating. No strategy is recommended unless it maintains a compliance score above the threshold. High recovery paired with high risk is flagged and excluded.

Constraint enforcement. Debtor profiles have hard limits (e.g., "never more than R$80/month"). If the Collector breaches a constraint, the simulation is flagged as unreliable.

Methodology designed for the Will Bank post-liquidation collection context. Adaptable to any multi-profile collection operation.

Why constraints matter
Constraints are hard behavioral invariants — the debtor will never cross them regardless of pressure. If a Collector breaches a constraint (e.g., pushes past R$80/month), the simulation data is flagged as unreliable. This ensures only realistic outcomes inform the playbook.

Strategy Toolkit
13 strategies span five approach categories — from empathetic vulnerability triage to assertive one-shot settlements
Each strategy is defined by 7+ behavioral dimensions: tone, opening approach, negotiation tactic, escalation style, concession willingness, compliance adherence, and follow-up. Every strategy uses boleto registrado and proactive liquidation disclosure.

Empathetic & Protective

Rapport-first approaches. Motivational interviewing, gain framing (CPF preservation), flexible installments aligned to income cycle. No escalation.

Strategies: Empathetic Payment Plan · Problem-Solving Callback · Blocked Balance Hardship Plan · Overindebtedness Stabilization

Trust & Legitimacy

Lead with identification, BCB liquidation disclosure, liquidator name, and official channels. Defer collection until trust is established.

Strategies: Liquidation Explainer · Consignado Confirmation · Reimbursement Milestone Callback

Assertive & Direct

Loss-aversion framing, one-shot settlement offers with deadline, gradual escalation. Minimal discovery questions. For debtors who can pay and respond to urgency.

Strategies: Assertive Settlement · Neutral Reminder

Digital Self-Service

WhatsApp-native, brief, pre-approved options via link. Maintains Will Bank's original voice. For digital-native debtors who prefer autonomy.

Strategies: WhatsApp Self-Service · Low Digital Access Guidance

Specialized Referral

Recognizes debtors who need institutional support: superendividamento audiências (Procon/Defensoria), cashflow-aligned plans for micro-merchants, guided channel navigation.

Strategies: Superendividamento Referral · Micro Merchant Cashflow Alignment
Behavioral Economics Foundation
Strategies are grounded in evidence-based framing:
Gain framing — "Preserve your CPF score" outperforms "avoid consequences"
Loss aversion — "This discount expires" for settlement-ready profiles
Implementation intention — "I'll call Tuesday at 7pm" creates commitment
Default bias — One recommended option reduces decision paralysis
Universal Safeguards
• Every strategy requires strict CDC/FDCPA compliance
• All payments via boleto registrado only
• Proactive liquidation disclosure on every contact
• No requests for card data, Pix, or passwords

Evaluation Framework
Seven dimensions capture the full picture — payment probability ranks strategies, compliance score gates recommendations
The independent Judge evaluates each simulated conversation without knowing which strategy was deployed. This blind evaluation mirrors how a compliance auditor would assess a real call — judging what happened, not what was intended.
Ranking Logic
Primary: Payment probability drives strategy ranking
Gate: Compliance score < 0.8 → strategy excluded for that profile
Tie-break: When two strategies can't be statistically separated after max repetitions, both are reported as tied — no false winner

Payment outcomes are ordered: full payment → partial payment → payment plan → promise to pay → no commitment → refusal → hang up. The Judge classifies each conversation into one of these seven outcomes.

Payment Probability
Estimated likelihood debtor would actually follow through on commitment
Compliance Score
Adherence to CDC, FDCPA, and fair collection regulations
Gate: ≥ 0.8 required
Debtor Satisfaction
How the debtor experienced the interaction — affects long-term recovery
Rapport Built
Quality of relationship — predicts callback success and plan adherence
Escalation Risk
Risk of complaint, lawsuit, Procon filing, or brand damage
Exclusion if > 0.3
Efficiency
Number of turns to reach outcome — fewer is better for both parties
Compliance exclusion is per (profile, strategy) pair — a strategy can be excluded for one profile and recommended for another.
Evaluation dimensions and thresholds from project specification. Scoring uses structured JSON output from the Judge agent.

Recommended Path Forward
Begin with a focused pilot across 5 profiles and 4 strategies — then scale to the full 14 × 13 matrix
Phase 1 — Validate

Pilot with highest-risk profiles

Run simulations for Cooperative Hardship, Scam Suspicious, Hostile Avoidant, Young First Card, and Over-indebted Chronic against the 4 core strategies. Validate that simulation results match domain expert expectations.

Phase 2 — Expand

Full matrix with statistical rigor

Scale to all 14 profiles × 13 strategies with sufficient repetitions per cell for statistical significance. Run across multiple AI model combinations to control for model-specific bias.

Phase 3 — Deploy

Playbook delivery and collector training

Generate the production playbook with per-profile strategy recommendations, sample scripts, and compliance guardrails. Train collection teams on profile-specific approaches.

Phase 4 — Iterate

Continuous refinement from real-world feedback

Feed real collection outcomes back into profiles and strategies. Add new debtor archetypes as they emerge. Evolve strategies based on regulatory changes and recovery data.

Expected Impact
↑ Recovery rate
Match each debtor to the strategy with the highest payment probability — not the one-size-fits-all default
↓ Compliance risk
Prevent dangerous strategy × profile mismatches before they reach a customer. Eliminate assertive tactics on vulnerable populations.
↑ Debtor satisfaction
Profile-appropriate approaches improve debtor experience — leading to higher plan adherence and fewer Procon complaints.
